## About Us

At NTUR LAB, we reject the illusion that performance requires dependency. We believe that true digital capability begins with absolute sovereignty.

For too long, the narrative has been that building cutting-edge artificial intelligence and high-performance data systems requires surrendering control—over our infrastructure, our data, and our digital future. We are here to dismantle that assumption.

We don’t build hype; we build infrastructure. Brick by brick, we focus on high-impact engineering, rigorous research, and delivering real, measurable digital capability.

## Contribution to our projects

Building a truly sovereign and resilient data stack isn’t a solo journey—it’s a collaborative ecosystem effort. Whether you are a system architect looking to benchmark our performance, an enterprise leader seeking compliance-first infrastructure, or a developer eager to contribute to zero-dependency components, there are several ways to get involved:

- Explore the Architecture: Take a closer look at our decoupled design, component specs, and sovereign principles directly on our landing page.
- Test & Feedback: Join our early partner network to evaluate real-world use cases, stress-test streaming pipelines, and shape our technical roadmap.
- Connect with the Team: Reach out to discuss technical integrations, compliance requirements, or custom architectural fits for your enterprise.

## Contribution License Agreement

In this repository we're hosting our [Contribution License Agreement (CLA)](CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.pdf); a version in Markdown format is available [here](CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md).

In order to accept you contribution, you'll have confirm it via our automated CLA pull request verification.  
Alternatively you can [download](CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.pdf), sign and send it to [cla@ntur-lab.com](mailto::cla@ntur-lab.com).

> This is a legally binding document, so *please read it carefully before agreeing to it*.

The Agreement may cover one or more software projects managed by Us.

## How to Contribute

As owner, contributors and maintainers of NTUR LAB' projects, and in the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to respect all people who contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating documentation, submitting pull requests or patches, and other activities.

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of level of experience, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

In that very sense, we adhere to the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) code of conduct. By participating, you are expected to uphold this code.

> Please report unacceptable behavior to [code-of-conduct@ntur-lab.com](mailto::code-of-conduct@ntur-lab.com)

> If you want to contribute, please read carefully our contribution terms at [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
